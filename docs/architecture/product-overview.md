# Product overview

## Mission

Syndeocare connects healthcare professionals with clinics, hospitals, and
medical centers through a trust-first digital staffing platform.

## Core value proposition

**Empowering healthcare staffing through trust, speed, and smart digital
connection.**

## Objectives

- streamline temporary and permanent healthcare recruitment
- help clinics and hospitals find verified professionals on demand
- give professionals flexible work opportunities and career growth
- support secure and transparent communication, workflows, and payments
- build a regional Arabic-friendly healthcare staffing ecosystem

## Stakeholders

- **Platform owner:** Tahawul Tech
- **Healthcare professionals:** doctors, nurses, dentists, technicians, specialists
- **Clinics / hospitals:** employers posting vacancies, shifts, and contracts
- **Admin team:** verification, disputes, analytics, moderation, and operations

## Surface boundaries

- **Admin:** web only
- **Clinics / hospitals:** web and mobile
- **Professionals:** native mobile only

## MVP functional pillars

- registration and role-based onboarding
- manual admin verification
- profile and organization setup
- search and matching
- booking and hiring workflow
- direct messaging and notifications
- ratings and reviews
- optional payment and escrow support

## Non-functional expectations

- secure APIs and encrypted traffic
- scalable cloud-native backend
- Arabic and English support with RTL
- mobile-first usability
- analytics and operational visibility
